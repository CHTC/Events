If your job needs to run for days, this training is for you! Learn how to run workflows with checkpointing capabilities on the OSPool. In this training, we will walk through a hands-on example using GROMACS, a popular molecular dynamics simulation software, but no molecular dynamics knowledge is needed to participate.

Topics covered:
* When you should checkpoint your jobs
* How to implement exit-driven checkpointing in your scripts and submit files.
* Common mistakes and troubleshooting tips

### Prerequisites
* Basic understanding of HPC/HTC systems
* Experience with the command line
* Some familiarity with HTCondor is recommended but not required
